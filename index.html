AREAS OF CARE

Rheumatology and autoimmune conditions

Rheumatological disorders can affect the joints, muscles, bones and immune system. Some conditions commonly evaluated within this speciality include:

Inflammatory arthritis

  • Rheumatoid arthritis
  • Psoriatic arthritis
  • Ankylosing spondylitis
  • Other inflammatory joint disorders

Systemic autoimmune diseases

  • Systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE)
  • Sjögren's syndrome
  • Systemic sclerosis
  • Connective tissue disorders

Other rheumatological conditions

  • Gout
  • Vasculitis
  • Myositis
  • Osteoporosis
